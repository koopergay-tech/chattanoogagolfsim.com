The space it needs

Measure once. That's the whole job.

If the space fits, everything else is on us. You confirm these three numbers at booking — and if it truly won't work on the day, we reschedule you free.

  • 15 × 15 ft floor footprint
  • 10 ft ceiling ideal · 9 ft workable (taller players stay on irons)
  • 1 outlet within ~50 ft — the projector doesn't run on batteries
  • Yellow line around the mat — your caddie keeps spectators behind it

The people part

A caddie comes with it. Every time.

Your caddie stays at the simulator the entire event — running the rotation, helping beginners, keeping the swing zone clear. Not an emcee, no microphone, no hype.
