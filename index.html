FAQ

The questions every planner asks us.

How much space does the simulator need?

About 15×15 feet. Garages, living rooms, barns, ballrooms, and covered patios all work — send us your space when you book and we'll confirm the fit.

How tall do the ceilings need to be?

10 ft is ideal. 9 ft works, though taller players may stick to irons off the driver.

What about power?

We need one standard outlet within about 50 feet. The projector doesn't run on batteries. No outlet? Tell us at booking and we'll talk options.

Indoor or outdoor?

Both. Indoor setups work anywhere with enough room, and covered outdoor setups — tents, pavilions, porches, barns — are great too. The enclosure just needs cover from direct rain.

How far do you travel?

A 40-mile radius of downtown Chattanooga is included in every package — Chattanooga, Hixson, Ooltewah, Collegedale, East Ridge, Red Bank, Signal Mountain, Soddy-Daisy, Lookout Mountain (GA), Ringgold, and Fort Oglethorpe. Beyond 40 miles, travel is $1.50 per mile round trip — quoted up front when you book.

Who is the caddie?

Your caddie is a trained attendant (usually the owner) who stays at the simulator the entire event — running games, helping beginners, and keeping everyone safe. Not an emcee, no microphone, no hype.

Do you stay the whole time?

Yes — every package, every event. Your caddie arrives with the rig about an hour early, stays at the simulator from first swing to last, and handles breakdown after. We never drop equipment and leave.

How do the deposit and damage hold work?

50% deposit at booking, balance by card 48 hours before your event. We keep a card on file with a $500 damage pre-authorization — it's never charged unless something actually breaks.

What's the weather policy?

We're built for Chattanooga weather: indoor and covered setups go ahead rain or shine. And our guarantee is simple — if we can't set up safely, you don't pay.

Is it good for non-golfers?

Yes — the games are built for beginners. Closest-to-the-pin, longest drive, and target games need zero golf experience, and your caddie coaches every first-timer through their first swing.

Can two groups book the same day?

First paid deposit locks the date. The form is a request; the deposit makes it yours.

How fast can I book?

48 hours minimum. The balance is due by card 48 hours before your event anyway.

What if my space doesn't fit when you arrive?

Measure first: 15×15 ft and a 9–10 ft ceiling. You confirm it at booking; if it truly won't work we'll reschedule you free.

Can I pick a specific course?

43,000+ of them. Pebble Beach, St Andrews, whatever your group argues about.

Do you do custom branding for corporate?

We don't — but bring your own banners and decorate around the setup however you like. We handle the golf.
