Policies · Plain language

The fine print, without the fine print.

Everything below is restated in the rental agreement you sign at booking. No surprises is the whole brand — so here it all is, up front.

Booking & payment

A 50% deposit locks your date — the form on the site is a request, the deposit makes it yours. The balance is due by card 48 hours before your event. We keep a card on file with a $500 damage pre-authorization (it's a hold, not a charge — released after the post-event equipment check). Every booking names one responsible adult who signs the rental agreement and waiver at setup.

Cancellation & refunds

7 or more days out: full refund of your deposit, no questions. Inside 7 days: your deposit converts to a credit good for 12 months on any package. Every booking includes one free reschedule with at least 48 hours' notice.

Weather

Free reschedule with 48 hours' notice, or we move the setup indoors, into a garage, or under cover — your call. And the standing guarantee: if we can't set up safely, you don't pay.

Damage

Normal wear and tear is on us — always. Broken equipment from misuse or ignoring the safety rules is charged at actual repair cost, capped at the $500 pre-authorization unless the damage is willful. The caddie documents the setup with photos before and after every event, so there's never an argument about what happened.

Alcohol & safety

We don't serve or provide alcohol, and your party is welcome to have it. Three rules keep everyone safe: one person swings at a time inside the marked swing zone, everyone else stays behind the yellow line, and guests who've had too much graduate to the putting game. Minors are supervised within arm's reach. The caddie's call on safety is final — that's in the agreement, and it's the reason we can bring real clubs to real parties.

The Weekend (self-run rental)

Our one unattended product, and it has its own rules: renters are 25 or older with a government-issued ID on file, the equipment stays at the address on the agreement, and the card on file carries a $1,000 pre-authorization for the rental period. Indoor or fully covered use only, kept dry, powered down overnight. We photograph condition at drop-off and pickup. No subletting, no lending.

Travel

A 40-mile radius of downtown Chattanooga is included in every package — that covers the whole service area on the map. Beyond 40 miles, travel is $1.50 per mile round trip, quoted up front before you book. No surprise line items, ever.

Your space

You confirm three numbers at booking: ~15×15 ft of floor, 10-ft ceilings (9 ft works — taller players stick to irons), and one standard outlet within 50 ft. If the space truly won't work when we arrive and it was disclosed in good faith, we reschedule you free.

Privacy

The booking form asks for exactly what we need to run your event: name, contact, date, venue, and party details. We use it to respond, book, and show up — nothing else. We never sell or share your information, and you can ask us to delete it any time at hello@chattanoogagolfsim.com.

Questions about any of this? Call (423) 493-3568 — a person answers, and the person is the owner.
